Luc and I recently wrapped up our time in the inaugural Birdaro training
program hosted by the CSCCE, and for our graduation, we got to share what we’ve
been cooking up for the Kokkos Project.

As Kokkos continues to grow, we’ve realized that our "human infrastructure"
needs just as much attention as our performance kernels. We co-presented our
plan for the Kokkos Community Playbook—a new effort to centralize how we work,
lead, and welcome others into the fold.

This isn't a finished, static manual just yet; it's a work-in-progress that
we're building directly into the Developer Corner of our documentation at
kokkos.org. Our talk covered:
* The Vision: Creating a comprehensive handbook that makes it way easier for
  new contributors to get their bearings and start shipping code.
* Structure & Format: Why we chose to keep our community guidelines right
  alongside our technical docs so they actually stay relevant.
* What’s Next: Our roadmap for building out the content, maintaining it
  long-term, and socializing these new "rules of the road" with the rest of the
  Kokkos community.

It was a refreshing experience to step back from the technical side for a bit
and focus on how
we make the ecosystem more sustainable and inclusive for everyone.
