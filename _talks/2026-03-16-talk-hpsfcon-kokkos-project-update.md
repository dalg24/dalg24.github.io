For the second edition of the HPSF Conference, the community returned to
downtown Chicago to discuss the state of the high-performance software
ecosystem. In this session, I provided a comprehensive update on the Kokkos
Project's progress over the past year, reflecting on our growth within the
foundation and our continued mission to provide performance portability for the
world's largest supercomputers.
