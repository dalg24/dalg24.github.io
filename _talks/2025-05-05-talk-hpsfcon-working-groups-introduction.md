Delivered during the opening plenary of the inaugural HPSF Conference, this
presentation served as a key status update from the Governing Board on the
progress achieved since the foundation’s launch one year prior. I joined fellow
board members to highlight the growth of our community-driven ecosystem and
introduced the technical working groups designed to handle project
cross-cutting activities. The talk provided attendees with the necessary
roadmap to engage with specific breakout sessions, fostering the collaborative
governance essential for long-term software sustainability in the HPC
community.
