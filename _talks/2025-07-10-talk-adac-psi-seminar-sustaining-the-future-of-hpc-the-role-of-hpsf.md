I recently had the pleasure of co-presenting a seminar titled "Sustaining the
Future of HPC: The Role of the HPSF" at the Portability, Sustainability &
Integration seminar series, a key initiative of the [Accelerated Data Analytics
and Computing (ADAC) Institute](https://adac.ornl.gov). I spoke alongside my
colleague [Julien Bigot](https://work.julien-bigot.fr) from CEA in France.
Julien and I both serve on the [High Performance Software Foundation
(HPSF)](https://hpsf.io) governing board, and our talk highlighted the HPSF's
crucial role in ensuring the longevity and effectiveness of High Performance
Computing.
