I helped lead the tutorial, Portable GPU Acceleration of HPC Applications with
Standard C++. This session focused on leveraging ISO C++23 parallelism and
concurrency features to portably accelerate HPC applications on heterogeneous
systems (AMD, Intel, NVIDIA GPUs) without vendor-specific extensions. We
provided hands-on experience covering classic HPC patterns like
multi-dimensional loops, reductions, and overlapping MPI communication with GPU
computation.
