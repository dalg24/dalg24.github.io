I co-organized and led this "Birds-of-a-Feather" session for the Consortium for
the Advancement of Scientific Software (CASS). The event centered on a
strategic roadmap discussion for the Kokkos Ecosystem, moderated by Mike Heroux
(former Director of Software Technology for the US Exascale Computing Project). 

Serving as a panelist alongside the Kokkos leadership team, I engaged with the
international HPC community to align our long-term development goals with the
evolving needs of large-scale scientific applications.
