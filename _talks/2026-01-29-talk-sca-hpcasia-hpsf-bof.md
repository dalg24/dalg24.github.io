HPSF maintained a powerful presence throughout SCA/HPCAsia in Osaka this
January. On January 28th, fellow governing board member Julien Bigot and I
delivered a lightning talk at the Exhibitor Forum, highlighting the
foundation's mission to unify the software stack for the exascale era. This was
supported by a dedicated HPSF booth on the exhibition floor, expertly managed
by Chris Clark from the Linux Foundation with the help of numerous foundation
partners. The week showcased the depth of the HPSF ecosystem, with
well-attended tutorials for Kokkos, Spack, and OpenCHAMI proving the growing
international demand for our projects. We capped off the conference on January
29th with a dedicated Birds of a Feather (BoF) session; following a
presentation by Julien and myself, I joined a lively panel discussion moderated
by Todd Gamblin (HPSF Governing Board Chair) to discuss the future of
sustainable, open-source HPC software.
