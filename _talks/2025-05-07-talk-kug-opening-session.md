The 3rd KUG meeting brought together approximately 70 practitioners and
developers to discuss the evolution of the performance portability ecosystem.
As part of the Kokkos leadership team, I coordinated the opening sessions to
provide a comprehensive roadmap of the project’s technical and community
growth.

## Leadership Plenary & Technical Updates

### Welcome and Overview
I opened the meeting by welcoming our 70 attendees and setting the objectives
for the gathering, highlighting the project’s current milestones and the
importance of our growing user community.  
<https://hpsf2025.sched.com/event/223Od/welcome-and-overview-damien-lebrun-grandie-oak-ridge-national-laboratory>

### Update on Ecosystem and Community
Christian provided a strategic update on the broader Kokkos ecosystem, focusing
on community engagement and the foundation of our collaborative efforts.  
<https://hpsf2025.sched.com/event/223Oo/update-on-the-ecosystem-and-community-christian-trott-sandia-national-laboratories>

### Kokkos Core Update
In this technical deep-dive, I presented the latest advancements in the Kokkos
Core library, focusing on new features, performance optimizations, and the
roadmap for upcoming releases to support the evolving "hardware zoo."  
<https://hpsf2025.sched.com/event/223PP/kokkos-core-update-damien-lebrun-grandie-oak-ridge-national-laboratory>

### Kokkos Kernels Update
Luc concluded the leadership updates with a detailed look at Kokkos Kernels,
showcasing progress in performance-portable linear algebra and graph kernels.
<https://hpsf2025.sched.com/event/223Pd/kokkos-kernels-update-luc-berger-vergiat-sandia-national-laboratories>
