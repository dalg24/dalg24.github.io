It was a pleasure to return to Japan and lead a well-attended, full-day Kokkos
tutorial at SCA/HPCAsia in Osaka. Joined by Luc Berger-Vergiat from Sandia  and
team members from CEA France, Paul Zehner, Yuuichi Asahi, and Julien Bigot, we
provided a deep dive into data parallelism, memory spaces, and the
`Kokkos::View` abstractions essential for exascale performance. This engagement
was particularly timely as the Japanese HPC community prepares for the
transition to Fugaku-NEXT; we are eager to support researchers navigating the
shift toward GPU-heavy architectures by ensuring their code remains
performance-portable and future-proof.
