This talk, presented as part of the [inaugural CSCCE Birdaro Training Program
for Open-Source
Leaders](https://www.cscce.org/2025/07/01/the-birdaro-training-program-supporting-open-source-leaders/),
detailed the strategic decision by the Kokkos leadership to pursue a neutral
home for the project.

My colleague, Luc Berger-Vergiat, and I shared the challenges of expanding from
a single lab to a multi-institutional, international collaboration.
Specifically, we discussed how the end of the ECP umbrella necessitated finding
a durable, vendor-neutral structure. The talk highlighted our collaboration
with the Spack project and the subsequent formation and launch of the [High
Performance Software Foundation (HPSF)](https://hpsf.io), emphasizing the
benefits of consolidated governance for sustainability, vendor support, and
streamlined user engagement.

Presented during the session on Organizational Governance and Identifying
Structures and Processes to Support Open-Source Projects.
